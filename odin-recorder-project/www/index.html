Each clip is 1 second. Wait for the high beep, then say the word a moment after it, not with it. For noise clips just stay quiet and let the room be heard; change Environment in Setup between sessions.

ZIP layout: odin/odin.spk01.0001.wav, similar_words/, plus noise/, english_words/ and metadata.csv. Audio is 16 kHz mono WAV. In the app every clip is saved at once to one fixed folder (Android/data/com.odin.recorder/files/OdinDataset). Export ZIP packs everything and opens the share sheet. Each export holds all clips so far. Uninstalling the app deletes the clips, so export first. Don't press Clear all until the export is saved. Shortcuts: 1 odin, 2 similar word, 3 noise, 4 English word, Esc stops.
